    I'm an AI Systems Engineer and full-stack developer based in Dubai. I build software with language models inside it, and I care most about the parts around the model that make it reliable.

    My recent work is LLM applications end to end: multi-provider routing across Groq, Gemini and OpenRouter, structured output validated with Zod, tool calling, RAG and Playwright browser automation. I prefer enforcing correctness in the architecture (schemas, deterministic simulators, provenance checks) rather than relying on a prompt to behave.

    On the application side I work across React, Next.js and Flutter frontends, Node.js, Express and Django backends, and PostgreSQL, SQLite and Firebase data layers. I ship with tests: my two most recent projects carry 82 and 126 test files.
